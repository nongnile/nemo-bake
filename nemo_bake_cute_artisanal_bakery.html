📖 Our Sweet Story

From a Sunny Kitchen to Your Daily Sweet Moment

Nemo Bake started in a cozy home kitchen when Nemo perfected her signature brown butter cookie recipe after months of baking experiments. We believe in slow baking: browning real French butter until nutty and caramelized, sourcing premium Japanese Uji matcha, and layering Maldon sea salt flakes over rich chocolate chunks.

Every single cookie is scooped by hand and baked fresh in small batches every morning. No preservatives, no shortcuts—just pure melt-in-your-mouth joy.
